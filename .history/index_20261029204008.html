What actually is "Space"

Space is basically everything that resides outside of Earth's Atmosphere. There is no air in space so you cannot breath and there is also no sound either because sound needs air to travel for those who did not know. Space starts at approximately 100 km (62 miles) above the Earth. This is called the Kármán line.

Space is SUPER cold in most places but if you are in the sunlight it can get VERY hot. Space is also almost just empty with darkness all around, but not totally empty. There is a little gas and dust floating around in space and possible debris and other things. Plus there is a lot of radiation in space that can be harmful to humans, which is why we need to wear special suits and helmets when we go into space.

The universe is around 13.8 billion years old, which is MUCH more than you think. Scientists think that it started with the Big Bang, which is a scientific phenomenon that happened a long time ago. The universe is also expanding, which means that it is getting bigger and bigger over time.

The SUN

The Sun is the biggest star and it is the closest star to Earth. It is about 150 million km away from Earth and light from the sun takes around 8 minutes to get to Earth, which is honestly pretty fast. The Sun is a star that is made up of mostly hydrogen and helium (He).

The Sun is extremely big and around 1.3 million Earths can fit inside of it. THINK OF THAT! In the middle of the sun hydrogen turns into helium and this process is called nuclear fusion. This process releases a lot of energy and that is what makes the sun shine so bright. The Sun is also very hot and the surface of the sun is around 5,500 degrees Celsius (9,932 degrees Fahrenheit) and the core of the sun is around 15 million degrees Celsius (27 million degrees Fahrenheit), which is incredibly WILD.

Do not look at the sun...trust me.It can hurt your eyes and possibly damage them permanently if you do for too long.

The PLANETS

There are 8 planets in our solar system and here they are in order from the sun:

Planet Type Moons One year
Mercury Rocky 0 ~88 days
Venus Rocky 0 ~225 days
Earth Rocky 1 ~365 days
Mars Rocky 2 ~687 days
Jupiter Gas Giant 95 ~12 Years
Saturn Gas Giant 146 ~29 Years
Uranus Ice Giant 28 ~84 Years
Neptune Ice Giant 16 ~165 Years

(The number of moons for the planets change sometimes because scientists find new ones, so the numbers may not be exact all the time.)

Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet and its closest to the sun. It has no air so it is extremely hot in the day and very cold at night.

Venus

Venus is the hottest planet. It has thick clouds which actually traps heat and it can reach to about 465 degrees Celsius.

Earth

Earth is where we live (unless you dont lol) It is the only planet that we know has life and around 71% of the Earth is covered in water.

Mars

Mars is known as the red planet because of rust in the dirt and it has the biggest volcano in the solar system which is called Olympus Mons.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the biggest planet in the solar system and it has a giant storm called the Great Red Spot. The storm is bigger than Earth, which is crazy.

Saturn

Saturn has big rings that are made of ice and rock and it is the least dense planet in the solar system.

Uranus

Uranus is tipped over on its side so it kind of rolls around the sun and it is a bluish green colour because of the methane gas in it.

Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun. It has the Fastest winds in the solar system.

What about Pluto??

Pluto used to be a planet but in 2006 scientists changed it to a dwarf planet because there are many planet-like objects in the solar system that look like it.
